Say Hello To Callie, I got a proper domain too! - Entry Date: 6th October 2026

First, I've decided that I am going to make a Webcomic! So say hello to Callie and Selina!

If you're wondering, yes, it's heavily inspired by Eric Shwartz' Sabrina Online, but hear me out! Gonna do something different!

To get ideas for the topic of a strip, i'll get a random word and make an entire comic strip based off of it! I've given
myself a bunch of words as tests and I was able to successfully come up of potential comic strips!

Occasionally, I may get an idea that I deem to be good enough to draw regardless of what word gets picked out.
For these, I'll give them the [S] flare. Normal strips done via a word picker will have the [W] flare.

If you like it, i'd love for you to help it spread! Feel free to put the Callie/Online web button on your website!

Go check out Callie/Online!

Second, I've finally decided to grab myself a proper .com domain! Using a github.io address to me
doesn't (as much) scream that it's a personal site, you know?

...Plus, second advantage: It can be accessed on old browsers too! For this reason I will be gradually updating
parts of the site to be more compatible with old browsers. Examples will include things like the lesser use of the
*.PNG file format.

While attempting to access the yourpalaxl.github.io URL results in an immediate redirect, I would highly
suggest you update the URLs accordingly to direct to http://www.yourpalaxl.com/ in the unlikely event
you have my site's URL or it's web button present on yours...
