Going on a break. I need it. - Journal Date: 11th December 2025, 01:04:27
The following originated from my FurAffinity journal section. It's here for archival purposes.

Hello Everyone.
This is the first time I've ever resorted to this in my ~5 years of being an internet artist, but I think it's time. As much as it pains me to say, I will be going on a small break from art altogether, I feel as if it's for my own health.

In recent months, my anger and stress levels have been building up constantly. I used to be excited to start doodling, and now? I just dread it. I dread it to the extent where I can't even bring myself to do any personal art. Yesterday the dam that's been holding everything back started to crack, resulting in me deleting some art, and cancelling/refunding all of my commissions. While a night's sleep did help a little afterwards, it didn't do much, as the crack was still very much present. Today, I finally snapped and broke down in a bout of extreme anger. My computer mouse had gotten obliterated in the process.

I've not stopped feeling like utter crap since. So me and other's have come to the conclusion that I just need a break. The break being indefinite is unfortunately not out of the question.

You may be asking what this will mean. Here's a quick summary.
-All pending commissions have been cancelled. All clients have been refunded.
-New Commissions and YCHs are on hold. Meaning that for now, all commission requests will be rejected.
-Unless I have a spike of art motivation, you will not be seeing any new art from me.
-For how long this break lasts has not been decided. Unfortunately, Indefinite is now out of the question.
-If I decide that it's time to leave art stuff behind for good, I will not delete anything for archival purposes.

Stay safe out there, and depending on how long I take, I hope you have a good Christmas and happy new year.
-Axl
