Now, about my stop on the information superhighway. So why did I make the jump to Github pages? Well when a website builder is constrained by modern "design standards", a lot of freedom and creativity is lost. Especially when it comes to things like Carrd. I wanted freedom. I wanted as much freedom as what things like GeoCities had given you in terms of designing your website, when you were free to express your creativity. It was then I was told about the services Neocities and Github Pages. After trying them both, I had decided to stick with Github Pages. With my love of retro, I decided to get to using Microsoft FrontPage on my old Windows XP machine. Where I made a retro themed website to show my art, and my other stuff properly, without constraints.

As I keep forgetting that my BlueSky exists, I will be working on trying to post to BlueSky more.
