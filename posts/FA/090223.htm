Art is temporarily halted... - Journal Date: 9th February 2023, 17:38:19
The following originated from my FurAffinity journal section. It's here for archival purposes.

I really wished I didn't have to post this, but I'm sorry to everyone who is waiting for art from me. Due to unforeseen circumstances regarding hardware (that occured last night), I am now unable to start or finish any new artworks. It is unknown how long this halt will last for. My sincere apologies.

What happened?
A couple of months ago, my drawing tablet had stopped charging. I knew the battery weren't the cause. I removed the backplate, and it turned out the charger port was completely snapped off of the board. I have been using my phone for art ever since. That was until last night. I picked up my phone, and several glass shards fell off the screen, the bottom of the phone was smashed somehow. I turned the screen on and all i saw was black with a vertical yellow line coming from the notch. I now have no hardware to draw with, unless you count in my laptop trackpad.

I promise those whom are waiting, I WILL straight away resume the queue when I am able to draw digitally again.

I will still be open for chats anywhere I can be found at!

Thanks for understanding!
