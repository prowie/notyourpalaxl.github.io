...Even the unusual bits.

Compared to the last time, i've finally decided what I should do! I've decided to go grayscale, with FireAlpaca as my strip making program.
I've given GSPaint a shot and as much as I love making art on the IIGS, making a comic strip definitely isn't the way to go.

I know how I'll get ideas for the comic strips. Quite simple, get a random word and make an entire comic strip based off of it! I've given
myself a bunch of words and were able to successfully come up of potential comic strips! Occasionally, I may get an idea that I deem to be good enough to draw regardless of what word gets picked out. For these, I'll give them the [S] flare. Normal strips done via a word picker will have the [W] flare.
